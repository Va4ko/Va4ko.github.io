BUILT FOR EVERYDAY PARKING

Useful information at the right moment.

ParkMe combines location-aware parking assistance with simple tools that help you handle paid parking and get back to your vehicle without unnecessary steps.

◎

Paid zone awareness

Stay informed when you enter or leave supported paid parking zones, with parking information designed to be easy to understand at a glance.

✉

Prepare parking SMS

ParkMe can prepare the relevant parking SMS details for supported zones. You stay in control and explicitly send the message yourself.

▣

CarPlay experience

Access parking-focused information and actions through the supported CarPlay experience, helping you keep attention where it belongs.

P

Remember your parked car

ParkMe can automatically remember where you parked your vehicle, with manual controls available whenever you want to save or update the location.

↗

Walk back to your car

See your parked vehicle on the iPhone map and request a walking route back to it when you're ready to return.

◷

Parking session at a glance

Keep an eye on an active parking session with convenient countdown information and supported Live Activity experiences.

DESIGNED TO ASSIST, NOT DISTRACT

Parking tools that stay out of your way.

ParkMe is a parking assistant. It does not find available parking spaces and its CarPlay experience is focused on parking rather than turn-by-turn vehicle navigation.

Location information is used to provide relevant parking features. You can learn more about how data is handled in the Privacy Policy.
